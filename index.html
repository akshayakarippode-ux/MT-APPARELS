Our Manufacturing Process

STEP 01

Design & Mockup

We translate your branding guides and blueprints into highly accurate digital mockups for initial visual alignment.

STEP 02

Fabric Selection

We source only custom woven filaments, density weaves, and certified ergonomic/antimicrobial fabrics.

STEP 03

Printing & Decoration

Fabric elements undergo high-heat dye sublimation or density multi-head embroidery using golden threads.

STEP 04

Precision Tailoring

Professional uniform tailors cut and secure each panel using heavy reinforced double stitching lines.

STEP 05

Quality Assurance

Every piece undergoes a multi-point inspection checks (dimensions, sewing resilience, print opacity) before packaging.
